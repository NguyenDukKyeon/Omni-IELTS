## Pipeline and authority

`canonical evidence/errors → deterministic WeaknessProfile → canonical Focus →
Frozen Assessment → Targeted Diagnostic`

An independent advisory lane may produce
`AdvisorySignal → AdvisoryWeakness → coaching Focus`, but it never enters the
canonical WeaknessProfile by repetition or model agreement.

- Same canonical inputs/version must rebuild the same profile and reason codes.
- Missing/sparse/conflicting evidence yields explicit uncertainty, not a weak,
  mastered or ready assertion.
- Due review always has precedence; neither focus lane calls a provider when
  Today opens.
- Targeted Diagnostic is intentionally biased toward weaknesses and cannot be
  named Mini-mock, representative assessment or band/readiness evidence.
- Assessment producers, including AI, cannot authorize keys, scoring or evidence.
