## URL research boundary

Any later pilot must be opt-in and private by default, enforce scheme/redirect,
address and byte/time limits, sanitize active content, preserve original URL and
retrieval provenance, and separate fetch success from license/answer authority.
`@mozilla/readability`, DOMPurify, `jsdom` or a companion browser remain research
candidates only; no library is selected here. Authenticated/private pages,
paywall bypass and production browser automation are outside the initial pilot.

## Mini-mock honesty boundary

A future Mini-mock must bind one immutable representative blueprint, exact
source/key/scoring/calibration versions, timer/accommodation policy and terminal
result. Readiness is an estimate with uncertainty and evidence provenance, not
an official band promise. AI cannot generate or approve the independent
blueprint/key, calibrate itself or infer readiness from coaching.

Neither deferred ID may appear in canonical package/status/dependency tables
until a separately approved reconciliation change set passes its re-open gate.
